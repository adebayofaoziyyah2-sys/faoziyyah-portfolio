About Me

Learning in public, building with purpose.

I’m Adebayo Faoziyyah, a Computer Science student at the University of Ilorin. I enjoy turning curiosity into capability — whether that means cleaning a messy spreadsheet, building a clear chart from a confusing table, or reading about a field of technology I haven’t tried yet.

My main interest right now is data analysis (especially Excel and data visualization). Beyond that, I’m deliberately keeping the door open: I want to explore widely before I specialize, and this portfolio will grow as I do.

I also care about people and purpose. I hope to one day contribute to humanitarian and global development work such as that of the United Nations, and I’m building my confidence in public speaking so I can share ideas clearly. These are aspirations I’m working toward through my studies — not qualifications I claim to have yet.

I’m looking for learning opportunities, mentorship, student communities, and beginner-friendly projects where I can contribute and improve.

Completed 01

Employee Records Data Cleaning in Excel

Objective: turn a messy 21-column raw employee sheet (duplicate headers, mixed date and salary formats, combined address field) into a clean, filtered, analysis-ready table with summary breakdowns.

What I did: standardized headers and formats in a dedicated Clean Sheet (filter A1:K82: Employee ID, names, department, salary, dates, address parts, demographics, job details); added a cleaned salary column; split the address; applied Excel filters; built PivotTable-style summaries (Sum of Salary, record counts, Average of Net Pay by year, age band, and employee).

Workbook at a glance: Raw Sheet A1:U83 → Clean Sheet filter A1:K82 → summary tables on Sheet1. Full figures are inside the file — download it to inspect every table.
